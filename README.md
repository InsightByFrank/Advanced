## 📖 Description  
This project presents a robust and interactive Excel-based dashboard built using advanced tools such as Power Pivot, VBA Macros, Power Query, and structured ELT processes. It visualizes global sales and product performance for Adventure Works — enabling business stakeholders to monitor trends, drive efficiency, and uncover strategic growth opportunities.

Designed for sales leaders, product managers, and business analysts, this solution turns raw transactional data into actionable insights on revenue, profitability, product line trends, and customer behavior.

## ⚙️ Excel Features & Tools Used  
- **Power Pivot Data Model** – Integrated fact/dimension tables (Sales, Products, Geography, Customers)  
- **DAX Measures** – Revenue, Gross Margin %, Return Rate, Customer Lifetime Value  
- **Power Query (ELT)** – Automated loading, transformation, merge & cleanup  
- **VBA Macros** – Auto-refresh dashboard, snapshot generation, date filter automation  
- **Pivot Tables/Charts** – Drilldowns by geography, product line, and time  
- **Named Ranges & Slicers** – Dynamic filtering by region, product category, channel, time frame

## ❓ Business Questions Answered  
- Which product categories generate the most global revenue?  
- How do revenue and order trends vary monthly and quarterly?  
- Which regions and countries show sales underperformance?  
- What is the profitability of each product line?  
- How do repeat customers influence long-term revenue?

---

## 📌 Observations & Insights  
- 🇺🇸 USA, 🇩🇪 Germany, and 🇬🇧 UK lead in total revenue and sales volume.  
- 🚲 Mountain Bikes and Road Bikes are the most profitable categories.  
- 🌐 Online orders generated over 60% of total revenue.  
- 🌍 South America has low return rates but also low sales — growth potential.  
- 🔁 Repeat customers make up nearly 50% of high-value purchases.
